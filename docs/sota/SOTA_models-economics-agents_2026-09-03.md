### A2. The two-gear split

**Gear 1 — local, resident, $0 marginal.** One always-on `llama-server` (b9627 or later) with `-np 2..4 -cb --cache-ram 8192 --kv-unified --cache-reuse 256 -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0 -fa on`, optionally `--slot-save-path` for disk persistence of slot KV. Measured here: a 1,664-token shared prefix re-sent with `cache_prompt: true` re-prefilled **1 token in 16 ms** instead of 1,664 tokens in 332 ms; a Qwen3-1.7B draft over Qwen3-8B reached 70.8% acceptance (216/305) for only +8% generation speed on this shared GPU; n-gram speculation (`--spec-type ngram-simple`) accepted 11% and gave +5%. Use this gear for triage, chunked reading, first-pass card extraction, and local screenshot OCR-ish reads. Model choice on 16 GB: Qwen3.5-9B Q5 (6.1 GiB) for throughput, Qwen3.8-27B at IQ3_XXS/Q3_K_XL (10.9–13.1 GB) for quality, Gemma 4 26B-A4B IQ4_XS (13.6 GB, 3.8B active) for speed with 256K context. Q4_K_M 27B files (16.5 GB Qwen3.8-27B, 18.3 GB Gemma 4 31B) do **not** fit resident on 16 GB.

**Gear 2 — API, cached, fan-out.** Sonnet 5 as the default worker (cache reads $0.20/MTok, 5-minute or 1-hour TTL), Opus 5 for synthesis, DeepSeek V4 Flash / GLM-5.3-Flash for anything that is volume-bound rather than quality-bound. With N parallel workers sharing one 100K-token prefix (§B4), Sonnet 5 costs $0.43 for ten workers with caching vs $2.00 without; DeepSeek V4 Flash costs $0.03 either way. Route by need: quality → Opus 5 / GPT-5.6 Sol; >256K single-shot → Gemini 3.6 Flash; cheapest → DeepSeek off-peak.

### B3. Local inference on a 16 GB consumer GPU

**llama.cpp features (verified against `llama-server --help` of b9627 on this machine, 2026-09-03, and the [master server README](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/tools/server/README.md)):** `--cache-prompt` (default on), `--cache-reuse N` (KV shifting), `--cache-ram N` host-memory prompt cache (default 8192 MiB; `-1` unlimited; `0` off — introduced in [PR #16391](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/16391), merged 2025-10-09, RAM-only, prefix-similarity hot-swap across "extra slots"), `--kv-unified` (default enabled, requires cache-ram), `-np/--parallel` + `-cb/--cont-batching`, `-sps/--slot-prompt-similarity` (default 0.5), `--slot-save-path` + `/slots/{id}?action=save|restore|erase` for disk persistence, `-ctk/-ctv` KV quantisation (q8_0, q4_0, iq4_nl…), `--swa-full`, `--ctx-checkpoints`, `--mmproj` for vision, `--reasoning on|off|auto` + `--reasoning-budget`. Speculative: `--spec-type` accepts `draft-simple, draft-eagle3, draft-mtp, ngram-simple, ngram-map-k, ngram-map-k4v, ngram-mod, ngram-cache` in b9627; master's [speculative.md](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/docs/speculative.md) adds `draft-dflash` and `draft-dspark` (DeepSeek V4's built-in DSpark heads; Qwen3-backbone). Draft flags: `-md`, `-ngld`, `--spec-draft-n-max` (default 3). The [host-memory caching "tutorial" discussion #20574](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions/20574) (2026-03-15) was flagged by commenters as AI-generated with non-existent flags — do not cite it.

Reading: prefill on this card is compute-bound at roughly 5–7K tok/s for 4–9B dense models and ~1.5K tok/s for a 27B dense model regardless of quant, so a 100K-token document costs ~20 s (9B) or ~65 s (27B) of prefill; generation is bandwidth-bound (≈450 GB/s effective: 4.68 GiB × 89.7 tok/s). A resident 27B at IQ3_XXS (10.9 GB) should therefore generate ~40 tok/s and a Q3_K_XL (13.1 GB) ~33 tok/s — **bandwidth-derived estimates, not measured**.
